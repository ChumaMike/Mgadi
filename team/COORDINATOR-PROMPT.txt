PRODUCT CONTEXT
Mgadi is a no-install, phone-first emergency web app for a South African small-business owner or bookkeeper in the first hour after a suspected payment-diversion/supplier-bank-details scam. Example: an email says the supplier changed accounts, the business pays, and the real supplier says the money never arrived. The response gap is deciding the right next action and coordinating responders under pressure.

Product line: “The first hour, guided.” We aim to reduce confusion and delay, support stopping a suspicious payment before it moves, contain suspected compromise and preserve a useful incident record. We cannot promise prevention or recovered money. An owner-reported checkbox is not proof of a completed bank call or outcome.

CURRENT CORE (VERIFY IN CODE)
- Plain-language incident input; optional names to hide.
- Pattern-based redaction before the server/optional AI, repeated on the server. Names are hidden only if supplied. Redaction is not guaranteed anonymisation.
- Five labels: payment_diversion, mailbox_compromise, suspicious_message, not_cyber, unsure; validated label + confidence only.
- Rules fallback. Optional AI adapter exists; no live AI evaluation has been established. The new Vercel adapters default to rules only.
- Persistent bank action and official local contact directory, then supplier verification, mailbox containment and evidence steps.
- Local SHA-256 file fingerprinting, downloadable JSON/print pack and checksum verifier. Evidence bytes do not upload. HTTPS/localhost is required for browser hashing.
- A checksum detects differences against a retained value, not origin, truth or a digital signature. Someone can edit content and recompute the checksum.
- Pack includes the original story and optional business details. It is not anonymous and does not contain the original evidence files.
- Original test baseline was seven automated tests and seven fixed synthetic routing cases. Deployment adapter tests were added during handover; rerun and record the current count. Do not call synthetic smoke scores real-world accuracy.
