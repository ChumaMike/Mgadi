WHAT THE SERVER KEEPS (short version; the long one is LINKS-DATA.md)
Per link, for at most 7 days (default 168 hours, clamped to 1-168): locked content it cannot read (AES-GCM, done in the browser; the key lives only after the # in the link), two SHA-256 fingerprints of link codes, a kind, a status (approved / rejected / acknowledged), and server times. Nothing else: no plaintext, key, raw code, account, name, address or log from Mgadi's code. The rate limiter holds a caller's address in process memory for up to an hour. The host and the store provider may log on their own. No "delete now".
