EVIDENCE · YOUR CHOICE

What access will Mgadi have?

Mgadi does not receive general access to your phone, folders, camera, microphone or messages when you accept the evidence prompt. You choose individual files in your browser.

If you choose Accept

  • Your browser opens its normal file chooser. You decide which screenshots, emails, PDFs or other supported files to select.
  • Mgadi reads only the selected files in your browser to calculate a SHA-256 fingerprint (a digital identifier).
  • Mgadi adds the filename, file size, file type, fingerprint and time of selection to your current incident record.
  • Files are not automatically uploaded. Their original contents are not included in the generated incident pack.

What Mgadi cannot do through this permission

  • Read unrelated files or browse your device in the background.
  • Record your screen, phone calls or microphone audio.
  • Contact your bank, police or anyone else on your behalf.
  • Recover deleted files or verify whether evidence is genuine just from a fingerprint.

What happens next?

You can deny or dismiss the prompt and continue your emergency steps. You can still choose evidence files manually later. Before downloading or sharing an incident pack, Mgadi shows you a review screen where you can choose which details to include.

Privacy reminder: Your original files remain on your device; keep backups safely. If you later share the downloaded pack, its included filenames, fingerprints and other selected details can be viewed by the recipient. Never include passwords, PINs or one-time codes.
